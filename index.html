Technical Architecture for pizzagalleria.in

How This Dashboard Integrates Into Pizza Galleria's Website

This system is engineered strictly as an internal franchise management portal, accessible by company leadership, regional operations managers, and authorized franchise partners.

1

Protected Subdomain / Route

Hosted under a secure company route such as https://ops.pizzagalleria.in or https://pizzagalleria.in/partner-portal, protected by Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

2

Automated POS Ingestion

Connects directly to existing cloud POS systems (Petpooja, Posist, UrbanPiper) via webhooks to automatically capture paid tickets, cancelled orders, and nightly closing stock counts.

3

100-Point Scoring Engine

Evaluates Economics (35), Demand (25), Operations (20), and Compliance (20) nightly, automatically classifying stores into Accelerate, Repair, or Review rails without manual spreadsheet errors.
